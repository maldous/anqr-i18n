About ANQR

What is ANQR?

ANQR (Animated QR) is a professional, free online tool for creating stunning animated and customized QR codes. Unlike traditional QR generators, ANQR allows you to embed images, GIFs, and custom designs directly into your QR codes while maintaining full scannability.

Our Mission

We believe QR codes don't have to be boring black-and-white squares. Our mission is to make QR code creation accessible, creative, and fun for everyone—from individual users to businesses looking to enhance their brand presence.

Key Features

  • Animated QR Codes: Upload GIFs to create eye-catching animated QR codes that stand out.
  • Image Overlays: Embed logos, photos, or any image into your QR codes using our advanced halftone technology.
  • Multiple Styles: Choose from square, rounded, dots, diamond, and connected module styles.
  • Full Customization: Control colors, error correction levels, sizes, and more.
  • High Error Correction: Our QR codes use advanced error correction to ensure they remain scannable even with significant customization.
  • Free to Use: All features are completely free. No registration required.
  • Privacy First: All processing happens in your browser. Your images never leave your device.
  • Export Options: Download as PNG, GIF, SVG, or WebP.

How It Works

ANQR uses sophisticated algorithms to modulate the size and appearance of QR code modules based on your overlay image. By leveraging QR code error correction capabilities, we can create visually striking designs while ensuring the code remains fully functional.

The halftone technique, similar to newspaper printing, varies the size of each module based on the brightness of the underlying image—creating the illusion of a full image within the QR code structure.

Technology

ANQR is built as a modern single-page application that runs entirely in your browser. We use:

  • Pure JavaScript with ES modules for performance
  • HTML5 Canvas for real-time QR code rendering
  • Client-side GIF parsing for animation support
  • Responsive design for all devices

Support the Project

ANQR is free and supported by non-intrusive advertising. By using our service, you help us maintain and improve the tool. If you find ANQR useful, please share it with others!
